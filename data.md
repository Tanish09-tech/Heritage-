# Sanskriti Suraksha - Indian Living Cultural Heritage Knowledge Base

## Overview
Sanskriti Suraksha is an AI-powered national living heritage preservation platform dedicated to safeguarding endangered Indian folk traditions, oral art forms, classical performing arts, and indigenous festivals across Indian states.

---

## 1. Living Traditions & Cultural Knowledge Chunks

### Q1: What is Powada and who are its master practitioners?
- **Tradition**: Shahiri Powada (शाहीरी पोवाडा)
- **Category**: Oral Ballad & Folk Music
- **State & Region**: Maharashtra (Pune, Satara, Kolhapur)
- **Primary Language**: Marathi (मराठी)
- **Master Practitioner**: Shahir Tukaram Jagtap (28 Years of Shahiri Akhada & Daf tradition)
- **Apprentice Learner**: Aniket Deshmukh
- **Threat Status**: CRITICAL (Threat Score: 32/100)
- **Cultural Origin**: Developed in the 1630s during the rise of the Maratha Empire under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The earliest recorded Powada was composed by Shahir Agandas in 1659 commemorating the victory at Pratapgad Fort.
- **Instruments**: Daf (circular drum), Tuntuna (single-string instrument), Manjira (cymbals).
- **Marathi Summary**: शाहीरी पोवाडा ही महाराष्ट्राची छत्रपती शिवाजी महाराजांच्या काळातील वीररसप्रधान लोककला आहे. शाहीर तुकाराम जगताप हे याचे प्रमुख गुरु आहेत.

### Q2: What is Warli Art and its sacred cultural significance?
- **Tradition**: Warli Tribal Painting & Tarpa Ritual (वारली चित्रकला)
- **Category**: Indigenous Tribal Wall Art & Performance
- **State & Region**: Maharashtra (Palghar, Dahanu, Thane)
- **Primary Language**: Varli Dialect / Marathi
- **Master Practitioner**: Sushma Vangad (Master Warli Artisan)
- **Threat Status**: CRITICAL (Threat Score: 38/100)
- **Cultural Origin**: Ancient tribal art form using geometric shapes (circle representing sun/moon, triangle representing trees/mountains, square representing sacred land) painted with rice paste on cow dung walls.
- **Instruments**: Tarpa (sacred wind instrument made of dried gourd and bamboo).
- **Marathi Summary**: वारली चित्रकला आणि तारपा नृत्य ही पालघर जिल्ह्यातील प्राचीन आदिवासी निसर्गपूजक परंपरा आहे.

### Q3: What is Baisakhi and Gatka martial art in Punjab?
- **Tradition**: Baisakhi & Gatka Martial Art (बैसाखी व गतका)
- **Category**: Spring Harvest Festival & Sikh Martial Heritage
- **State & Region**: Punjab (Amritsar, Anandpur Sahib)
- **Primary Language**: Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ)
- **Master Practitioner**: Ustad Harinder Singh (32 Years of Gatka Shastar Vidiya)
- **Apprentice Learner**: Simran Kaur
- **Threat Status**: VIBRANT / STRONG (Threat Score: 86/100)
- **Cultural Origin**: Baisakhi marks the golden Rabi wheat harvest and the creation of Khalsa Panth by Guru Gobind Singh Ji in 1699. Gatka features wooden Soti sparring, swordplay, and Nagada drum rhythms.
- **Punjabi Summary**: ਵਿਸਾਖੀ ਅਤੇ ਗਤਕਾ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਅਮੀਰ ਵਿਰਾਸਤ ਅਤੇ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ ਸਾਜਨਾ ਦਿਵਸ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਕ ਹੈ।

### Q4: What is Bhavai Folk Theatre and Garba of Gujarat?
- **Tradition**: Bhavai Folk Theatre & Navratri Garba (ભવાઈ અને ગરબા)
- **Category**: Folk Street Theatre & Circular Festival Dance
- **State & Region**: Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Patan)
- **Primary Language**: Gujarati (ગુજરાતી)
- **Master Practitioner**: Pandit Raghunath Joshi (25 Years of Bhavai Vesha)
- **Apprentice Learner**: Aarav Patel
- **Threat Status**: VULNERABLE / STRONG (Garba UNESCO listed)
- **Cultural Origin**: 14th-century folk theatre founded by Asaita Thakar. Veshas involve high-pitch singing, string instruments, and social satire. Garba celebrates Goddess Durga's divine feminine power.
- **Gujarati Summary**: ભવાઈ અને ગરબા ગુજરાત સંસ્કૃતિના અમૂલ્ય લોકનાટ્ય અને ઉત્સવ નૃત્ય છે.

### Q5: What is Koodiyattam Sanskrit Theatre of Kerala?
- **Tradition**: Koodiyattam Sanskrit Theatre (कूडियाट्टम)
- **Category**: UNESCO World Masterpiece of Temple Theatre
- **State & Region**: Kerala (Thrissur, Palakkad)
- **Primary Language**: Sanskrit & Malayalam (മലയാളം)
- **Master Practitioner**: Guru Manikandan Nair (30 Years of Koodiyattam)
- **Apprentice Learner**: Meera Menon
- **Threat Status**: CRITICAL (Threat Score: 32/100, only 4 living masters)
- **Cultural Origin**: World's oldest surviving Sanskrit theatre tradition (2,000+ years old) performed inside temple Koothambalams using intricate eye movements (Nethrabhinaya) and Mizhavu copper drums.
- **Malayalam Summary**: കൂടിയാട്ടം 2000 വർഷം പഴക്കമുള്ള യുനെസ്കോ അംഗീകൃത സംസ്കൃത നാടക കലയാണ്.

### Q6: What is Rongali Bihu of Assam?
- **Tradition**: Rongali Bihu & Bohag Utsav (ৰাঙালী বিহু)
- **Category**: Spring Agricultural New Year Festival
- **State & Region**: Assam (Jorhat, Guwahati, Majuli Island)
- **Primary Language**: Assamese (অসমীয়া)
- **Master Practitioner**: Shrimati Hemlata Gogoi (22 Years of Bihu Dance)
- **Apprentice Learner**: Bishal Saikia
- **Threat Status**: STRONG (Threat Score: 88/100)
- **Cultural Origin**: Marks Assamese New Year and spring seed sowing. Features Muktoli dance in Muga silk Mekhela Chadors, powered by Dhol, Pepa (buffalo horn trumpet), and Gogona (bamboo jaw harp).
- **Assamese Summary**: ৰঙালী বিহু অসমৰ জাতীয় উৎসৱ আৰু বসন্তৰ নতুন বছৰৰ আনন্দৰ প্ৰতীক্ষক।

## 2. Platform Architecture & AI Capabilities
- **Voice Recognition (STT)**: Web Speech API listens to speech input in English, Marathi, Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati, Malayalam, and Assamese.
- **Dynamic Retrieval & Chunking**: Matches recognized keywords against the `data.md` knowledge base.
- **AI Text-to-Speech Engine**: Speaks out the retrieved details in the selected state language with high clarity.
- **Mandatory Profile Protection**: Persists user registration details permanently in database to ensure seamless re-logins.
